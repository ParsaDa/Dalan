# Dalan 0.6.0: UI and motion design brief (for the design chat)

Status: final, agreed with the owner on 2026-11-20. This supersedes `design/2026-11-20-ui-0.6.0-brief-draft.md` (kept as the decision log).

## 1. What Dalan is and what you are designing
Dalan is a web panel that runs and manages VPN/proxy servers (a fork of 3x-ui, runs Xray-core, plus tunnel engines BackPack and Rakhsh, Tor and PIA exits). It exists to get past internet censorship in Iran. Release 0.6.0 is a complete redesign of the whole panel plus a new multi-node experience.

Deliver a design the owner and his engineers can build on the current stack (React 19, Ant Design 6 with a deep custom theme and CSS, Vite, TypeScript; no Tailwind, no rewrite). Nothing in the backend or the stack changes for the design.

## 2. Users and devices
- The owner, and admins who sell accounts to end users. The project will go public, so a non-technical admin must understand every screen without help.
- Phones and laptops are equal first-class targets. Phone layout is not a shrunk desktop.
- Default language English. Persian and RTL must work everywhere (14 locales exist; text length varies a lot). Mirror layout and motion in RTL.
- Slow links and weak phones are common. See the effects rules in section 5.

## 3. Reference and mood
- References the owner loved: the "Quantix" crypto dashboard (dark, soft glow, glass sidebar, live status strip, cards with coloured edge and sparkline, pills, filter chips), a smart-home board with big frosted tiles over a soft backdrop and a header with time and status, and a smart-home app with a big greeting, room chips, a narrow icon rail, power toggles and gradient sliders on tiles.
- Mood: calm, premium, alive. Glass and soft light, not neon. Information first; the effects sit behind the data.
- Take the spirit, do not copy any of these screens or their art.

## 4. Colour (final: palette C "Saffron and ink")
Dark first, light mode equally designed.

| Role | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #111114 | #F4EFE6 (paper cream) |
| Surface | #1B1B20 | #FFFFFF |
| Text | #F4EFE6 | #111114 |
| Muted text | #A8A399 | #6B665C |
| Brand (saffron) | #FFB020 | #FFB020 as fill with ink text; about 28% darker (#B87D00 range) for thin lines, bars, text |
| Secondary | #34D399 (mint) | #34D399, darker for text |
| Glass fill / border | white 7% / white 13% | white 60% / ink 11% |

Status colours are fixed and never follow the accent. Every status also has an icon and a word, never colour alone.
- Success: #34D399 dark, #047857 light.
- Warning: #FF7A45 dark, #C2410C light (moved to orange-red because brand saffron and a yellow warning looked the same).
- Danger: #F87171 dark; light needs a value of about #B91C1C (designer to confirm contrast).

### Per-node accent
- Two layers. The brand colour (saffron) is the accent on "This server", the logo and the login page. Each node has its own accent that replaces the brand colour only in the frame: active tab or chip, primary button, progress bars, card edges, sparklines and charts, and it tints the background glow.
- About 12 curated accents, readable on dark and light, no pure green (clashes with success). Examples already tried: Turkey rose-red #FF3D6E, Germany #FFC83D, Netherlands #FF8A3D, France #4C8DFF, UK #9B8CFF, lime #B6F23C.
- Default accent comes from the main colour of the node's flag; the admin can override it from the palette or a custom colour.
- Text on an accent fill uses a computed on-accent colour (ink or white) so contrast holds on yellow and on rose. In light mode, accents used for thin lines and text are darkened automatically.
- Open for you: Netherlands orange sits close to the warning orange; review it.

## 5. Glass, background and effects
- Cards are glass on every page: translucent fill, blurred backdrop, thin light border, large radius (about 18px on tiles).
- The page background moves slowly: soft colour glows drifting behind the glass, tinted by the active node accent. No photo backdrops (weight and licensing).
- Dense screens (client tables, logs, config editors) use a more opaque glass so long text stays readable.
- Effects levels, with the user's switch "Effects: Full / Light / Off":
  - Full (default on capable devices): drifting glows, full blur, all motion.
  - Light (auto-selected on weak devices, also manual): static glows, small blur, only essential motion.
  - Off: solid cards, no blur, no ambient motion. Also forced by the system "reduce motion" setting.
- Ambient animation pauses when the tab is hidden. Text contrast must pass in all three levels and both themes.

## 6. Layout
- Left icon rail (collapsible to icons) with grouped sections and a glowing active item; becomes a bottom bar on phones.
- Header: big greeting ("Good evening, name"), one status line ("This server, all systems healthy, updated 12s ago"), search, effects and theme switches.
- Node chips or tabs under the header. Tab 1 "This server": its own dashboard on top and, below it, a summary card for each connected node (status, latency, CPU, memory, traffic, Xray state; data already comes from node heartbeats). Then one tab per node, labelled with the node name or the admin's chosen country plus an SVG flag. Selecting a node switches the whole panel into that node (dashboard, inbounds, clients, tunnels, outbounds, Xray restart, logs, Tor, engines, settings), as if its own panel were open. On phones the tabs become a scrollable chip row with a "+N" menu with search.
- Tile-based dashboard. Order on "This server": 1 server health, 2 tunnel status, 3 usage and online users, 4 alerts.
- Tiles show state directly: toggles for on/off things (Xray, tunnels), gradient bars for CPU/memory/quota, pills for status, sparklines for trends.
- Pages that all need the new look: dashboard, inbounds and clients (tables with filter chips and badges), tunnels (BackPack and Rakhsh only; the Xray reverse tunnel engine is removed in 0.6.0, so no Xray option in create/import forms), outbounds and routing, nodes, Xray config editor, logs, Tor and PIA windows, settings, login, and every modal and empty state.
- Nodes get a new user-set Country field (name, flag, optional accent override) in the add/edit node form.
- Use SVG flags everywhere. Windows does not render flag emoji; this includes the Tor and PIA windows.

## 7. Graphic design (make it special)
Use strong graphic design, not just a restyled component library:
- A real brand mark and wordmark for Dalan, with an animated version for loading and login.
- A consistent custom icon set or a heavily tuned one (stroke weight, corner radius) so icons feel like the brand.
- Illustrations for empty states, login, error pages and first-run, in the saffron-and-ink language, working in dark and light.
- A distinctive data-viz style for sparklines, usage charts and node health (glow, gradients, rounded caps) that follows the node accent.
- Optional idea for you to judge: a stylised world map with node dots on "This server", where the node accent lights the dot.
- Typography with a clear hierarchy that also works for Persian script; propose Latin and Persian font pairing.

## 8. Motion and animation (this is a core part of the brief)
Design a full motion system, not decoration. Every switch, load and state change is animated with intent, with a real polished animation language.

Required moments:
- Switching node tabs: the accent colour sweeps through the frame, the background glow changes tint, and tile content cross-fades or slides in a short stagger. It should feel like moving into another server, not reloading a page.
- Page and route transitions, panel and modal open/close (shared-element where it helps), drawer on phones.
- Loading: branded loader, skeleton screens with shimmer for tiles, tables and charts, progress for long actions (installing an engine, updating a panel, Xray restart) that shows real stages.
- Live data: numbers count smoothly to new values, sparklines extend in real time, status pills pulse softly on change, online/offline transitions are obvious but calm.
- Controls: toggles, buttons, chips, sliders, copy-to-clipboard and save confirmations have small tactile micro-interactions; success and error feedback is animated and also text-based.
- Entrance: tiles rise in with a short stagger on first load.
- Ambient: the slow drifting background (section 5).

Rules for the motion system:
- Define tokens: durations (fast, base, slow), easing curves, stagger step, and which properties may animate (transform and opacity first; avoid layout-thrashing properties).
- Target smooth 60fps on mid-range phones; heavy effects drop away under "Light" and "Off".
- Respect "reduce motion": replace movement with instant or fade-only changes.
- Motion mirrors in RTL (slides and sweeps reverse direction).
- Keep the UI usable while animating: no waiting on animation to act, interruptible transitions.

## 9. What to deliver
1. Design tokens (colour, type, radius, spacing, elevation, blur, motion) for dark and light, plus the accent derivation rule.
2. Component specs for the Ant Design 6 theme and the custom pieces (glass tile, node chip, rail, header, status pill, toggle, bar, sparkline, table with chips, modals, empty states).
3. Screens for every page in section 6, dark and light, laptop and phone, LTR and RTL, with the three effects levels noted where they differ.
4. The motion spec (section 8) with timings, curves and storyboards or a prototype for: node switch, page transition, loading, live update, entrance.
5. Brand mark, icon set direction, illustration set, chart style.
6. Hand-off notes for engineers: which pieces are plain CSS, which need JS, and what to cut first if performance is poor.

## 10. Out of scope for 0.6.0
Packet-loss based switching, a fourth tunnel engine, a redesigned Xray reverse tunnel (removed now, redesigned in a later release).
